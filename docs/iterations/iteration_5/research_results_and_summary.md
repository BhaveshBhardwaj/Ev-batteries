## 1. Executive Summary

Iteration 5 delivered **Module M07 (`calibration`)**, an autonomous closed-loop current sensor self-calibration engine designed for India's 2-wheeler and 3-wheeler commercial EV fleet.

Low-cost BMS hardware across Indian commercial fleets relies on uncalibrated resistive shunts or open-loop Hall-effect current sensors subject to severe thermal drift ($\pm 0.2\text{ A}$ to $\pm 1.0\text{ A}$) and aging-induced gain scaling errors ($\pm 2\%$ to $\pm 6\%$). In standard Coulomb counting, an uncalibrated $0.65\text{ A}$ offset causes unbounded error accumulation ($> 15\text{ Ah/day}$), rendering state-of-health tracking and remaining useful life estimation completely useless.

M07 resolves this without requiring laboratory test benches:
1. **Electrochemical Rest-Anchor Offset Regression:** Isolates zero-bias DC offset ($\hat{\alpha} = \frac{1}{N_{rest}} \sum I_k$) from resting intervals where true current is physically constrained to $I_{true} = 0$.
2. **Dual OCV Equilibrium Anchor Matching:** Couples initial and post-drive/charge rest periods across significant throughput ($|\Delta \text{SoC}| \ge 8\%$). Tail sampling eliminates transient $RC$ overpotentials ($V(t) \to \text{OCV}$), allowing direct regression of sensor gain error:
   $$\hat{\beta} = \frac{Q_{meas} - \hat{\alpha}\Delta t}{Q_{nom} \cdot \Delta \text{SoC}}$$
3. **Gate 2 Sensor Fault Diagnostics:** Enforces physical hardware safety thresholds ($|\hat{\alpha}| > 1.50\text{ A}$ or $|\hat{\beta} - 1.0| > 0.08$), flagging defective analog front-ends or degraded shunts.
4. **Drift-Free Calibrated Telemetry:** Generates corrected series $I_{corr} = (I_{meas} - \hat{\alpha}) / \hat{\beta}$ with verified $\ge 90\%$ drift elimination.

## 2. Physics-Based Simulation & Ground-Truth Validation

The module was validated against synthetic sessions generated by [tests/synthetic/telemetry_generator.cpp](file:///c:/Users/bhard/OneDrive/Desktop/Ev-battery/tests/synthetic/telemetry_generator.cpp), which implements a physical $R_0 + R_1 \parallel C_1$ equivalent circuit, Safari & Delacourt (2011) electrochemical OCV, Joule heating, and ambient heat dissipation under $42^\circ\text{C}$ Indian summer ambient:

### 2.1 Multi-Stage Operational Cycle Parameter Recovery
- **Hardware Drift Injected:** Shunt bias $\alpha_{true} = 0.65\text{ A}$, gain error $\beta_{true} = 1.015$ ($+1.5\%$), current noise $\sigma = 0.15\text{ A}$, terminal voltage noise $\sigma = 0.03\text{ V}$.
- **Operational Profile:** Rest (5 min) $\to$ Dynamic City Drive (20 min) $\to$ Rest (6.7 min) $\to$ CC Fast Charge (30 min) $\to$ Post-Charge Rest (5 min).

| Parameter / Metric | Injected Ground Truth | M07 Regressed Value | Recovery Error | Acceptance Criterion | Status |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Zero-Current Bias ($\hat{\alpha}$)** | $0.6500\text{ A}$ | **$0.6483\text{ A}$** | $0.0017\text{ A}$ | $|\hat{\alpha} - \alpha_{true}| \le 0.05\text{ A}$ | **PASSED ($29\times$ margin)** |
| **Gain Scaling Factor ($\hat{\beta}$)** | $1.0150$ | **$1.0142$** | $0.0008$ | $|\hat{\beta} - \beta_{true}| \le 0.015$ | **PASSED ($18\times$ margin)** |
| **Raw Coulomb Cumulative Error** | $0.0000\text{ Ah}$ | **$0.7224\text{ Ah}$** | $+0.7224\text{ Ah}$ | — | Baseline uncalibrated drift |
| **Calibrated Coulomb Cumulative Error**| $0.0000\text{ Ah}$ | **$0.0481\text{ Ah}$** | $+0.0481\text{ Ah}$ | Drift reduction $\ge 90\%$ | **PASSED ($93.3\%$ reduction)** |

### 2.2 Commercial Depot Overnight / Idle Drift Elimination
- 2-hour parking dwell ($7,200\text{ s}$) with $+0.80\text{ A}$ shunt offset.
- Raw Coulomb counting recorded **$1.60\text{ Ah}$** of phantom energy accumulation.
- M07 autonomous calibration eliminated the phantom drift to **$0.000\text{ Ah}$ ($> 99.9\%$ reduction)**.

### 2.3 Gate 2 Physical Fault Triggering
- Injected catastrophic offset ($2.20\text{ A} > 1.50\text{ A}$): correctly triggered `sensor_fault_detected = true` and `ALERT_SENSOR_OFFSET_EXCESSIVE`.
- Injected shunt degradation gain error ($1.12 > 1.08$): correctly triggered `sensor_fault_detected = true` and `ALERT_SENSOR_GAIN_EXCESSIVE`.
